Questions about the training

Fees, accreditation, entry and online study.

What does the teacher training cost?

Fees vary by intake and format, and there is no early-bird price. Choose the intake you want in the enquiry form and we quote it.

Is the teacher training accredited?

Yes, by Yoga Australia. Graduates are eligible for professional indemnity and public liability insurance.

Do I need experience to start the training?

Entry is by application and an interview with the course director. Use the enquiry form and say how long you have practised.

Can I do the whole training online?

Yes. The online intake follows the in-person syllabus, with the same teachers.
