Who the course is for

Entry is by application and an interview with the course director.

You want to teach

No teaching experience is needed.

You want to study yoga in depth

The course is also for dedicated students seeking deeper knowledge.

What the 200 hours cover

  • Philosophy

    Patanjali's Yoga Sutras, the Hatha Yoga Pradipika and the Bhagavad Gita.

  • Asana

    More than 50 postures in eight categories, and Suryanamaskar with its mantra.

  • Anatomy

    The body's systems and alignment, with chakras, nadis and bandhas.

  • Pranayama

    Ujjayi, nadi shodhana, kapalabhati and sitali, with bandhas and mudras.

  • Meditation and mantra

    Dharana and dhyana, with mantra and Sanskrit terminology.

  • Teaching

    Running a class of 15 and a class of 50. Mock teaching with feedback.

Philosophy
Ashtanga and Hatha yoga, the Yoga Sutras, Sanskrit shlokas and mantras, the Bhagavad Gita, an introduction to the Vedas and Upanishads
Suryanamaskar
Levels and progressive sequences, with the mantra used in the practice
Asana

Nervous, endocrine, lymphatic, respiratory, cardiovascular, digestive and musculoskeletal systems; biomechanics and alignment
Yogic anatomy
Chakras, nadis and bandhas

Meditation

Business
Starting your own classes, promotion, applying for teaching work, mentoring from the school director

Questions about the Melbourne training

Do I need experience to do yoga teacher training?

No teaching experience is needed. Trainees are mentored from the basics, whatever their starting point. We ask for a commitment to attend every session.

Is the teacher training recognised by Yoga Australia?

Yes. It is a Yoga Australia registered course.

How long does the 200-hour training take?

Six months, on Sundays, so you can keep working. The exact schedule is confirmed when you enrol.

Can I teach yoga after the course?

Yes. You graduate with a 200-hour teaching certificate and are eligible for the insurance that gyms, community centres and studios ask for. Many graduates now teach in Melbourne, around Australia and overseas.

How much does the teacher training cost?

Fees vary by intake and format, and there is no early-bird price. Send an enquiry and we quote the Melbourne intake by email.

How is this school different?

We teach classical Ashtanga and Hatha from the source texts, without props. The principal teacher, Samir Rane, is a Registered Senior Teacher with Yoga Australia and the third generation of a yogic family.

Can I do the teacher training online?

Yes. The online intake is live-streamed, with the same teachers and syllabus.
